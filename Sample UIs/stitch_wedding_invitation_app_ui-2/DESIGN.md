## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, permanence, and bespoke artistry of haute stationery combined with the frictionless clarity of a premier digital planning suite. It moves beyond standard wedding software by treating the screen as fine artisanal paper, blending archival romance with modern editorial discipline.

### Aesthetic Movement & Philosophy
The visual direction fuses **Tactile Luxury Editorial** with **Soft Neoclassical Minimalism**:
- **Bespoke Stationery Tactility:** UI surfaces emulate archival 600gsm cotton rag paper, deckle-edge contours, blind debossing, and warm metallic hot-stamping.
- **Editorial Poise:** Generous negative space, asymmetric layouts, typographic weight interplay, and intentional stillness. Every screen feels curated rather than crowded.
- **Understated Ceremony:** Interactive feedback channels physical heirlooms—wax seal badges, micro-embossed toggles, hairline frame separators, and quiet metallic shimmers.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for discerning couples, bespoke event designers, and distinguished guests. The interface evokes:
- **Calm assurance:** Transforming multi-vendor, guest-list logistics into an orderly, serene ritual.
- **Heirloom sentiment:** Evoking the quiet weight of opening a wax-sealed invitation envelope.
- **Effortless refinement:** Ensuring high-utility workflows (RSVPs, registry balances, dietary tallies) never compromise romantic elegance.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from botanical flora, antique gold leaf, raw silk, and hand-milled rag paper. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#606C5A` - Muted Tuscan Sage):** Anchors actionable focal points, active states, and structural status highlights. It supplies organic grounding without visual harshness.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Brushed Antique Foil):** Used sparingly for ceremonial accents, active step badges, monetary details, monograms, and micro-delighters.
- **Tertiary (`#7D8B74` - Soft Laurel):** Supporting states, secondary badges, category filters, and decorative botanical divider accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#242220` - Warm Charcoal):** High-contrast editorial text. It provides the depth of vintage letterpress ink without the stark detachment of pure `#000000`.

### Backgrounds & Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Alabaster):** Natural daylight tone for mobile screens.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F5F1EB` - Cotton Ivory):** Layered card containers, bottom sheets, and input fields.
- **Surface Accent (`#E8DFD5` - Pale Champagne Parchment):** Selected states, interactive pill tracks, and divider bands.
- **Muted Neutral (`#4A4642` - Sienese Charcoal):** Secondary typography, captioning, and inactive metadata.
- **Subtle Outline (`#E3DBD0` - Deckle Border):** Soft, paper-like low-contrast borders.

## Typography

The pairing between **Playfair Display** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** reflects an intentional tension: high-society editorial literature meeting crisp modern utility.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Exclusively reserved for emotional anchors, couple names, venue headings, milestones, and chapter titles. Maintain italic flourishes for contextual subtitles (`font-style: italic`).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Form Controls & Badges):** Engineered for dense wedding data—dietary tallies, seat numbers, vendor contract clauses, and RSVP countdowns. 
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase metadata (`label-sm`, `label-monogram`) must carry deliberate letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) to evoke hand-set metal type. Never track out large Playfair Display headings beyond `-0.01em`.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined spatial cadence balances expressive luxury imagery with task-oriented planning screens.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile First (4-Column Layout):** 16px gutters, 20px outer margin. Content conforms to safe touch zones, using bottom sheets for complex configurations (guest editing, budget tracking).
- **Tablet / Expanded Mobile (8-Column Layout):** 24px gutters, 32px outer margin. Accommodates dual-pane planning interfaces (e.g., seating chart canvas alongside guest index).
- **Rhythm & Negative Space:** Every section is partitioned with intentional breathing room. Structural section dividers rely on `space-xl` gaps paired with hairlines (`#E3DBD0`), preserving breathing room between emotional storytelling and logistical entry.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on harsh drop shadows or neon blurs, depth evokes hand-assembled stationery layers, letterpress indentations, and light reflecting across metallic foil.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Raw Ground):** Base Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`). Flat canvas representing desk level.
- **Level 1 (Cardstock / Surface Containers):** Tinted Ivory (`#F5F1EB`) edged with a 1px border of `#E3DBD0`. Ambient shadow: `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards / Floating Menus):** Pure warm surface with stacked micro-diffusion: `0 4px 20px -3px rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.07), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.03)`. Border lightened to `#ECE5DC`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Ceremony Overlays):** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.12)`.
- **Debossed State (Letterpress Inset):** Form fields and toggle tracks simulate impression into cotton paper using an inset shadow: `inset 0 1px 3px rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.05)`.
- **Foil Highlight:** Gold elements utilize an ultra-subtle directional upper highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`), reinforcing the look of hot-stamped gold foil.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Soft Geometric Structure (`roundedness: 1`)**, reflecting precision-cut invitation envelopes and cards.

### Shape Language & Special Treatments
- **Standard UI Containers (Cards, Modals, Inputs):** Crisp 4px (`0.25rem`) to 8px (`0.5rem`) soft corners. Sharp enough to feel editorial and tailored, never bulbous or casual.
- **Wax Seals & Monogram Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) to simulate physical sealing wax stamps and metallic coins.
- **Deckle Edge Accent:** Specialized invitation cards feature subtle, micro-perforated or scalloped outer edge borders (`1px` dashed/fine-grooved borders using `#D8CEBF`) to honor bespoke papermaking.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Sage Ceremony):** Background `#606C5A`, text `#FAF8F5`, 8px corner radius, height 48px. Subtle inner top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Press state drops tone to `#4E5849`.
- **Secondary (Brushed Foil Accent):** Background `#FAF8F5`, border 1px solid `#C5A059`, text `#242220`. Hover/Focus applies a faint champagne wash (`#FAF5EE`).
- **Ghost (Editorial Text):** Zero background, typography `label-lg`, bottom hairline border on hover, text `#4A4642`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-height (32px), 4px radius. Unselected: `#F5F1EB` background, `#4A4642` label, border 1px solid `#E3DBD0`.
- Selected: `#606C5A` background with crisp white text, or pale champagne `#E8DFD5` with `#242220` text for RSVP status pills.

### Cards (Stationery Suites)
- Fabricated in `#F5F1EB` with a continuous 1px `#E3DBD0` border.
- Optional top gold hairline (`2px` solid `#C5A059`) for VIP vendor, premier tier, or bridal party highlights.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height 48px, background `#FAF8F5`, 1px solid `#DCD4C8`, 4px radius.
- Inset shadow `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(36, 34, 32, 0.04)`.
- Focused: Border transitions to `#606C5A` with a 2px outer aura in `rgba(96, 108, 90, 0.15)`. Label floats in `label-sm` with letter-spacing `0.08em` in `#606C5A`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 20x20px square with 3px soft radius, `#E3DBD0` border, filling with `#606C5A` and displaying an ivory serif-inspired check icon.
- Radios: Concentric rings. Outer ring `#DCD4C8`, inner indicator `#606C5A` separated by 2px ivory whitespace.

### Lists & Guest Registry Rows
- Borderless rows split by 1px horizontal hairline dividers in `#EFE8DE`.
- Left-aligned typographic block (Guest Name in `Playfair Display 16px`, RSVP/Meal in `Plus Jakarta Sans 12px`).
- Right-aligned status stamp with gold or laurel tinting.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Wax Seal Badge:** 36px circular stamped emblem in deep laurel (`#606C5A`) or antique gold (`#C5A059`) featuring embossed couple monograms.
- **Deckle Border Invitation Card:** Double-lined luxury frame with 4px margin between borders for RSVP previews and digital vow keepsakes.
- **Countdown Banner:** Split typographic matrix featuring numeric counters in `Playfair Display 32px` paired with micro labels in `label-sm` spaced at `0.1em`.
